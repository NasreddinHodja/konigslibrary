#+TITLE: Design system
#+OPTIONS: ^:{}

The rules the UI follows: meth.cat's small-web look (a bitmap font, one ink on a dark background, raised panels, dither), kept usable by the research in [[file:research.org][research.org]] and fixing what [[file:audit.org][the audit]] found. Settled in the showcase, =/showcase= under =bun run dev= (=src/routes/showcase/=), which builds every rule below and 404s in builds. Step 3 of the redesign in =TODO.org=; step 5 applies it to the app.

* Font

- WebPlus IBM VGA 9x16 everywhere (=static/fonts/WebPlus_IBM_VGA_9x16.woff=), from VileR's Ultimate Oldschool PC Font Pack, CC BY-SA 4.0. Shipping it needs credit to VileR with a link to int10h.org, on the About page.
- Two sizes, the font's own height and twice it, since a bitmap font is only crisp at those ([[https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/readme/][readme]]): 16px for everything, 32px for titles. Nothing is bold; the font has no bold. Font smoothing off (=-webkit-font-smoothing: none=).
- WebPlus covers extended Latin (Æther, Ōkami); Japanese titles fall back to the system's font.
- Lowercase words for everything the app says in its own voice: nav, buttons, labels, headings ("library", "resume ch. 12 p. 4"). Titles of manga and chapters keep their own case.
- Icons are bitmaps drawn for the app in =src/lib/ui/icons.ts=, all on one 9×9 grid with 1px lines and their padding kept, drawn pixel for pixel in the current colour by =Icon.svelte=. One grid means one pixel size: each is scaled by the whole number that brings 9px nearest its =size=, ×3 (27px) by default, ×2 in the cover badges. Empty states carry one above their words. Picked in the showcase over [[https://github.com/the-moonwitch/Cozette][Cozette]]'s glyphs, which came on grids from 1×5 to 11×11 and so drew at different pixel sizes; Cozette had won over GNU Unifont and Siji. Other marks are glyphs from the font: =►= current or an error, =›= a list item, =█░= progress.

* Colour

A palette is three colours, picked in Settings: background, text, ink. Everything else is mixed from them:

| token  | made of                                                       | for                                                                    |
|--------+---------------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =bg=   | background                                                    | panels, and text on ink                                                |
| =fg=   | text                                                          | text only: body, values, what's typed in a field                       |
| =dim=  | text at 67% over =bg=                                         | secondary text: hints, labels, counts                                  |
| =ink=  | ink                                                           | borders, links, buttons, and every state: current, selected, progress |
| =hi=   | ink 60%, toward white (black on a light =bg=); toward =bg= when that barely moves it | hover, the focus ring                           |
| =ink2= | ink at 60% over =bg=                                          | the first shadow step, the texture's light pixels; never text          |
| =ink3= | ink at 30% over =bg=                                          | the second shadow step, hover backgrounds, tracks, dotted leaders      |

- =fg= is for text and nothing else. A state drawn in =fg= jumps hue (a pale cyan text colour against a pink ink), so states stay in the ink.
- Hover moves ink to =hi=, a lighter shade of the same hue, never to =fg=.
- No success or error colour: an error is the ink with =►= in front, and a broken field gets a dashed border.

Presets, one bark the default; =custom= takes any three. Contrast against =bg=, computed from these values (text on ink or =hi=, as on a primary button, is the same ratio):

| preset    | bg        | text      | ink       |   fg | dim |  ink |   hi |
|-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+------+-----+------+------|
| bubblegum | =#000000= | =#ffe0f0= | =#ff8fc8= | 17.2 | 7.6 | 10.0 | 13.5 |
| paper     | =#f4f0e8= | =#141414= | =#141414= | 16.2 | 5.8 | 16.2 |  7.7 |
| one bark  | =#282c34= | =#dcdfe4= | =#c678dd= | 10.5 | 5.6 |  4.8 |  7.5 |
| white     | =#000000= | =#ffffff= | =#ffffff= | 21.0 | 9.1 | 21.0 | 11.5 |

Every text token clears 4.5:1 in every preset. =ink2= is under 4.5 in all but white and paper, which is why it's never text. A =custom= palette can fail; the showcase's colour panel shows its ratios.

The theme today has six colours (bg, fg, surface, border, muted, reader background); it becomes these three, and a saved theme carries over as bg, fg and border-as-ink.

* Panels and texture

- Content sits in panels: =bg= fill, 1px =ink= border, and meth.cat's stepped hard shadow, =2px 2px 0 ink2, 4px 4px 0 ink3=. The same shadow raises the primary button, dialogs, toasts and the reader HUD's panel. No soft shadows, no blur, no rounding.
- Behind the panels, a dither texture: soft clouds, ordered-dithered (Bayer 8×8) into =bg= and =ink2=, generated in the browser in the palette's colours, tiled, fixed, drawn pixelated.
- A dialog's backdrop is a 50% checkerboard of =bg= over the screen.
- Titles are 32px with a 1px ink rule under them; a heading inside a panel is 16px and underlined.

* Controls

** Buttons
All from one component. Primary and default are one height, 32px with a mouse and 40px on touch; text buttons are as tall as their line, so a heading with one beside it keeps its height. All get 48px to tap, via =hit=.

| kind    | rest                          | hover                          | pressed                                  |
|---------+-------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------------------|
| primary | =ink= fill, =bg= text, raised | fill goes to =hi=              | moves 2px into its shadow, one step left |
| default | 1px =ink= outline, =ink= text | inverts: =ink= fill, =bg= text | as hover                                 |
| text    | =ink= word, no box            | =hi=, underlined               |                                          |

- The primary is the one thing a screen is for, and the only raised button: resume on the manga page, log in, a dialog's confirm (delete, download), allow on a waiting login.
- A waiting login's button reads "allow 4821", the code in the label.
- Disabled is =opacity-40=; busy changes the label in place ("connecting…").

** Links
Standing alone ("← back", "how to use", "change server"): =ink=, no underline at rest; hover =hi= and underlined. Inside running text: underlined at rest too, since colour alone doesn't set a link apart from the words around it (axe's =link-in-text-block=).

** Fields
1px =ink= border, =bg= fill, =fg= text, =dim= placeholder, label above in =dim=. A field in error gets a dashed border and "► the message" in ink under it.

** Choices and navigation
- A choice of a few (device/server, ltr/rtl, turn/scroll): boxed cells side by side, the current one filled with ink; hover is an =ink3= background with =hi= text.
- A list of places (Settings' sections): boxed rows, =›= before each, the current one inverted with =►=.
- The bottom tab bar: three boxed words (library, upload, settings), the current inverted.

** Focus
A 1px dotted =hi= outline, offset 2px, on everything, fields included.

* Covers and chapters

- A cover has a 3px double =ink= frame; hover turns it =hi=. Current or selected: a solid 3px ink frame and an inverted caption.
- The caption is a band on the cover's foot: =bg=, an ink rule above, up to two lines.
- Badges are 24px squares in the corner with a glyph: =√= downloaded, =↓= download (a button, inverts on hover), an empty ink box unselected, =√= on ink selected. Downloading is a 4px ink bar across the top.
- A chapter tile has a 1px ink frame and a bar with the number and page count; the resume chapter's is a solid 3px ink frame with the bar inverted.
- A manga's facts are rows of name, dotted leader (=ink3=) and value; tags are a plain comma list.

* Progress and motion

- Download progress is text: =█= and =░= in ink, then "5/12".
- Waiting is a =|/-\= spinner, still under reduced motion.
- The reader's chapter progress is the HUD top bar's bottom edge: a 2px =ink3= track filled with =ink=.
- Nothing else moves except a pressed button sinking, and what already slides (the HUD, the tab pages); those keep =ANIM_DURATION= and go to zero under reduced motion.

* Layout

- Spacing from 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px.
- The library, manga page, settings and login are stacks of panels over the texture, 12px from the screen's edge on a phone.
- Settings ends with a footer: KONIGSLIBRARY at 32px in a box drawn with box-drawing characters (=╔═╗ ║ ╚═╝=), "by nas" under it in =dim=. Login has no wordmark.
- Touch targets as now: =hit= gives 48px on touch; nothing under 24px with a mouse.
